## What It Does

- **Real-time risk event correlation** — geopolitical, regulatory, supply-chain, and FX transmission signals converge into enterprise impact
- **Business line exposure scoring** — revenue-weighted risk quantification across product, market, and route dimensions
- **Supply chain chokepoint tracking** — 15+ critical maritime/logistics corridors with live disruption alerts
- **Compliance & regulatory radar** — CBAM, battery regulations, tariffs, customs rules with forward-looking timeline
- **Enterprise risk taxonomy** — 4 risk axes (geopolitical, regulatory, supply_chain, financial_fx) with cross-axis transmission mapping
- **Immunity scoring** — predictive resilience index for sourcing strategies and hedging decisions
- **Demo + live hybrid** — curated enterprise scenarios + real-time news/cluster feeds
- **Qwen agent-powered** — identification and transmission classification for every risk card
- **AI-native alerts** — department-aware action plans tied to business impact and exposure windows
